The Team

Four people, one mission: turn the joy of playing into real progress for medical research.

Emily Connor

President

A former hospital administrator turned advocate, Emily founded A Ball For All after witnessing how few resources reach adaptive-sports communities. She steers the organization's vision, partnerships and long-term strategy with one rule: no athlete is ever left on the sideline.

Richter Oogway

Head of Research

With two decades in biomedical engineering, Richter leads the research department and the Eye Ice program. He bridges the worlds of laboratory science and lived experience, working directly with end-users to design technology that truly belongs in their daily lives.

Devon Reese

Sport Director

A former Paralympic coach, Devon designs every tournament from the ground up — formats, classifications, safety, atmosphere. He ensures that competitions are fair, intense and unforgettable, both for the athletes on the court and the crowd in the stands.

Amar Zanardi

Ambassador

Athlete, speaker and storyteller, Amar is the public face of A Ball For All. He carries the organization's voice to schools, federations and media, transforming each event into a conversation about inclusion, innovation and the right to play.
